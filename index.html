Hey! I am Bethel Hall, a Ph.D student at Stevens Institute of Technology. I am super grateful to be advised by Prof. William Eiers. My research focuses on improving the reliability and correctness of generative AI (GenAI) systems through Neuro-symbolic methods. My work explores how formal reasoning and symbolic techniques can be integrated with large language models (LLMs) to guarantee their correctness and safety.
