Projects

Research systems that combine large language models with formal methods for software requirements, cloud security, and verifiable policy analysis.

VERIMED

We introduce a new approach that combines neurosymbolic methods with LLMs and SMT solvers for auditing ambiguous, inconsistent, and underspecified software requirements.

Natural-language requirements SMT Z3 Counterexample-guided repair
64/64 hemodialysis requirements formalized 55.4% to 98.5% verified QA accuracy with SMT counterexamples 12/64 ambiguity-sensitive requirements reduced to 0/64
Paper

CloudFix

Repairs misconfigured AWS IAM policies by pairing SMT-based fault localization with LLM-generated patches, then validating each candidate against must-allow and must-deny requests.

AWS IAM Policy repair Fault localization Quacky + Z3
282 real-world AWS IAM policies 84.0% full repair rate on 10-request tasks vs. 48.2% baseline 54.3% complete repairs on 30-request tasks vs. 22.3% baseline
Paper Code

Access-Control Policy Synthesis and Summarization

Studies whether LLMs truly understand cloud access-control policies and introduces an automata-based summarizer that converts policy behavior into verified, interpretable regular expressions.

Policy synthesis Semantic equivalence Automata Model counting
15.4% to 45.8% semantic equivalence for non-reasoning LLMs 86.4% to 93.7% semantic equivalence for reasoning LLMs 0.889 mean similarity for semantic request summaries
